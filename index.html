Our Story

A Streator Tradition.

Sody's has been the chair on South Vermillion Street for as long as anyone in town remembers. We're the kind of barbershop where the regulars know the order of operations, the newcomers find a seat, and everyone leaves looking sharp.

Walk-ins welcome any day we're open. Whether you need a clean fade, a beard tightened, or a fresh hot-towel cleanup, we take the time to do it right — never rushed, never cookie-cutter.

"Clean cut. Honest work. The same standard, every chair."
